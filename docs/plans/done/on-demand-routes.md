# Phase 12 — On-demand routes (ratified 2026-09-02; design in `docs/route-design.md`)

The second product feature: a route drawn for one person from what they said, at the moment they ask, over the cleaned network. **The network is the catalogue.** The pipeline stops at the network, its places and its starts, and exports them as one binary pack (`packs/<run_id>/network.npz` + manifest); the backend mmaps it and draws in-process with bounded scipy Dijkstra, using the same assembly rules, the same `ids.route_id` mint, the same schema and the same card as the 627. Measured basis (2026-09-01): GDS has no cost cutoff, so the bounded distance field every loop and out-and-back needs is 6.5 s streamed or 1–4 s pre-projected in Neo4j, against tens of milliseconds in scipy over a 35 MB pack; A→B in GDS is 23 ms and fine, but two geometric truths are one too many. Neo4j keeps meaning (places, names, favourites, map-back) and gives up geometry. The LLM boundary does not move: `OutingIntent` (activity, shape, party, fitness, hours, waypoints with roles, surface exclusions, setting, theme, start spec, sleep, area, `refine_from` ordinal) carries no query, template, id, coordinate or weight; `chat/compile.py` owns every number the planner sees; `here` is a typed `near` on `ChatRequest` that never reaches `extract_plan`. Infeasibility is a count ("42 loops in reach, all over 10 % asphalt"), the Clarify is built from the counts, chips send a typed delta straight to the composer with no model call.

Ratified (owner, 2026-09-02): ① no human review before a user sees a route — planner invariants (Phase 10 F1/F2 rules as pure functions), a 627-id parity test, a 3 × 3 km fixture pack, "report this route" on the card; ② cards show a duration labelled *our estimate* from `core/durations.py`, the schema still refuses the field; ③ drive time is pgRouting over OSM car roads in the existing PostGIS, at export time; ④ GDS retired — A→B on the pack too; ⑤ a shared package `shared/routes` (`vaivia_routes`: assemble, loops, destinations, document, ids) as an editable path dependency of both uv projects, tests in the pipeline CI job; ⑥ Neo4j heap max 1 g, tx-log retention 2 days, now.

Supersedes: Phase 7 P7; Phase 10 F0/F3/F4 as catalogue generation; Phase 9 D4 `publish-catalogue` → `publish-pack`; `docs/routing-engine.md`'s GraphHopper choice; `core/comfort.py` runtime plumbing (calibration moves into the pack's cost columns). Dies as product, stays as the parity oracle: `catalogue.route`/`route_edge`, the Neo4j catalogue load, `search_loops`/`estimate_loops`/`loop_candidates`/`loop_poi_conjunction`, `LoopSearchIntent`, `catalogue_view`, `draw/generate.py`. Golden `expect_loops` ids become `expect_facts` bands pinned to a pack `run_id`. Alternatives (leg graph, Neo4j field-and-stitch, sampler, mosaico, agentic tool loop) and the measurement table are in the design doc; the leg graph is the named fallback past ~500k edges.
